### Novel Value

- The report offers a useful synthesis that treats climate, warfare, mobility, trade, and political fragility as interacting processes rather than competing single causes.
- It usefully separates direct evidence of events from claims about their causal role, especially for drought, destruction, migration, and the Sea Peoples.
- It identifies causal ambiguity in trade disruption and institutional breakdown by treating them as possible causes, consequences, and amplifiers rather than assigning them a fixed role.
