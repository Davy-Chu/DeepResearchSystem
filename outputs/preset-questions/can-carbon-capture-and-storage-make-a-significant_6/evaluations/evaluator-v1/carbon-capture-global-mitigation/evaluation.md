### R8

- Coverage: 0.75
- Depth: 0.75
- Rationale: The conclusion is clear, conditional, and substantially synthesizes the report’s main dimensions, but the definition of significance and the evidence base are not sufficiently precise for a fully supported judgment.
- Candidate evidence:
  - It gives a clear conditional judgment: “Yes—but primarily as a targeted and complementary technology.”
  - It defines significance operationally as a possible “several gigatonnes of annual CO₂ reduction or removal later this century.”
  - It synthesizes sectoral applicability, energy penalties, costs, scale-up, storage, lifecycle emissions, alternatives, and the mixed power-sector record.
  - It states conditions including direct abatement priority, lifecycle accounting, permanent storage, restrictions on fossil expansion, and shared infrastructure.
  - It distinguishes demonstrated capabilities from future-dependent scale-up and notes uncertainties in costs, pipelines, storage, BECCS, and DAC.
- Missing:
  - The conclusion does not set a more precise timeframe or threshold for what counts as significant, especially for 2030 and 2050.
  - Its synthesis rests on largely uncited and mostly generic evidence rather than documented measured project results.
  - Key assumptions behind the several-gigatonne estimate—energy availability, storage development, biomass sustainability, policy, and alternatives—are not explicitly integrated into the judgment.
  - The report does not clearly state what level of contribution is feasible now versus expected only after future technical, financial, and regulatory developments.

### Novel Value

- The report offers a useful conditional synthesis: CCS is framed as potentially significant only as a targeted complement to direct emissions reduction, rather than as a dominant or fossil-fuel-preserving strategy.
- It usefully separates gross facility capture from lifecycle abatement and distinguishes fossil CCS from genuine carbon dioxide removal through BECCS or DAC.
- The report adds decision-relevant conditions involving permanent storage, lifecycle accounting, shared infrastructure, and prevention of unabated fossil lock-in.
