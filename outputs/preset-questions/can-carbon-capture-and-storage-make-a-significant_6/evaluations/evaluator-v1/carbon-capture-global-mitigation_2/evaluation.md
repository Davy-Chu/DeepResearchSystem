- Depth: 0.75

- Candidate evidence:

  - It could more explicitly separate what is feasible now, what is feasible by 2030, and what depends on future technological, infrastructural, and policy developments.

### Novel Value

- The report offers a useful synthesis centered on the distinction between high facility-level capture rates and lower full-chain net reductions.
- It presents a clear targeted-deployment thesis: CCS is most defensible for concentrated, hard-to-abate industrial emissions and carefully governed removals, rather than as a general license to continue unabated fossil-fuel use.
- It explicitly distinguishes theoretical geological storage capacity from characterized, permitted, accessible, and economically deployable capacity.
- It identifies the gap between announced project pipelines and operating capacity as a central scalability issue.
