- Candidate evidence:

  - It notes that capture efficiencies above 90% involve greater costs and energy requirements [S20].

  - It gives no operating periods, availability factors, capture-rate measurements, storage quantities, or net emissions reductions.

- Coverage: 0.25
- Depth: 0.25

  - It identifies high costs and limited storage sites as scalability constraints [Finding 2].

  - The “tenfold increase by 2025” claim is not contextualized against actual capacity or deployment outcomes.

  - The report does not weigh where opposing and supporting arguments apply differently across CCS pathways.
