**Success criteria:**

### SQ4 [SECONDARY]

**Question:**

What practical conditions determine whether synthetic data produces net benefits or risks in large language model training and fine-tuning?

**Success criteria:**

Synthesize findings by use case, synthetic-data generation method, mixture with real data, filtering or verification process, training stage, and deployment context, while clearly distinguishing training benefits from fine-tuning benefits and reporting uncertainty where evidence is limited.

**Initial status:** UNRESEARCHED

## Synthesis Requirements

- Integrate data quality, bias, and evaluation rather than treating them as independent dimensions.
- Distinguish benefits from risks and identify trade-offs, including when a mitigation in one dimension creates problems in another.
- Separate evidence about pretraining from evidence about fine-tuning and preserve relevant real-world or deployment context.
- Relate evaluation methods to the specific failure modes they can detect or miss, including risks from evaluating on data generated by similar models.

## Output Requirements

- Answer in terms of real-world risks and benefits of synthetic data for large language model training or fine-tuning.
- Focus explicitly on data quality, bias, and evaluation.
- Present uncertainty and limitations where the evidence does not support a firm conclusion.
